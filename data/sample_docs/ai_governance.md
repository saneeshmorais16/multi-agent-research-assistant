# AI Governance Notes

AI governance for assistant systems focuses on traceability, human review, risk controls, and transparency. Teams should keep records of prompts, retrieved evidence, model outputs, and reviewer decisions when systems influence important work.

Grounded systems reduce hallucination risk by requiring answers to cite trusted sources and by refusing to answer when evidence is missing. A critic step can evaluate whether claims are supported by citations and whether uncertainty should be shown.

Governance does not remove all risk. Teams should document limitations, evaluate failure cases, monitor outputs, and keep humans responsible for decisions that affect people.
